codicon.ttf is the font out of the package as it comes. codicons.hor is every icon's name and the number of its
character, written by Horizon/Art/make_codicons.py out of the package's codicon.css. Horizon draws them as a distance
field like the rest of its text (UISymbols), $(name) in a label.
